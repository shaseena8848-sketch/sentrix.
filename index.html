SOLUTIONS

Built around how each team is targeted.


Banking and financial services

Fake support handles ask customers for credentials. Workflow: watch for lookalike handles, review evidence, document for fraud teams.

E-commerce and retail

Cloned storefronts reuse product imagery. Workflow: flag lookalike domains and misused assets, then queue for review.

Technology companies

Unofficial apps borrow product names and icons. Workflow: compare publisher identity and permissions against your official listings.

Consumer brands

Giveaway scams hijack campaign identities. Workflow: track campaign names and escalate suspicious accounts.

Media and entertainment

Fake ticket and fan accounts spread fast. Workflow: prioritise by severity around launches and events.

Enterprises and security teams

Many signals, few analysts. Workflow: one queue, explained scores, audit trail, structured reports.

FAQ

Straight answers.


What is digital risk protection?

Finding and handling threats to your brand outside your own network: fake profiles, scam sites, and unofficial apps.

How does AI identify potential impersonation?

By comparing signals such as names, handles, imagery and wording against your brand, and explaining why something looks suspicious. It produces leads, not verdicts.

Can the platform monitor multiple social networks?

Coverage depends on connected sources, available APIs and permissions. This prototype connects to none.

Does it detect suspicious mobile applications?

It is designed to help investigate apps using available evidence. The prototype only evaluates text you type.

Are results verified or AI-generated?

AI-assisted results are indicators needing human review. Here, every result is simulated.

Can I integrate my own threat intelligence sources?

The scanner is structured around one analyze() function so a backend service can replace it. No integrations ship today.

How is submitted information handled?

In this prototype, scanner input stays in your browser. Workspace notes are saved in local storage only.

Does the platform automatically remove fake accounts?

No. Takedowns are not implemented. The platform helps you document evidence for reports to platforms or legal teams.
